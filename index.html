About Our College

Welcome to Modern College

Founded in 2015, Samesta College has been at the forefront of educational innovation, providing students with a transformative learning experience that combines academic excellence with real-world applications.

Our campus spans over 50 acres of state-of-the-art facilities, including advanced laboratories, extensive libraries, and collaborative learning spaces designed to foster creativity and critical thinking.
